SARAZA
The vision of Kohue Wines lived as a dream in our minds for many years before coming to life in 2018 when we began producing a limited collection of hand-crafted, high altitude mountain wines from the finest terroirs in the foothills of the Andes.

This majestic region offers a unique synergy that nurtures naturally healthy vineyards. The high altitude, distinct soil types, glacial waters, and dry microclimate, allow berries to develop an incredible complexity of aromas and flavors particular to this place in the Andes that captured our attention from the beginning.

With minimal intervention, we entrust nature to reveal the identity of each site, preserving the inherent qualities of the fruit. Grounded in years of winemaking experience and a deep respect for nature and its ecosystems, we craft singular wines from this region that are profound and elegant, offering depth, complexity, and the ability to age gracefully over the years.

Each year, our family embarks on a journey between two hemispheres, following the rhythm of the harvest, from Mendoza to Napa.

Grounded In Napa Valley
After nearly two decades of living between both regions and contributing to some of Napa’s most prestigious wine projects, a natural opportunity emerged in 2023 for us to craft our very own first Napa Valley Cabernet Sauvignon, one that felt deeply aligned with our history and values.

Maxi had already begun shaping his winemaking path in Napa when in 2007, he met Bart and Daphne Araujo. While working in Pomerol, he was invited to join their winemaking team in 2009 at the legendary Eisele Vineyard, formerly known as Araujo Estate. He later became part of the Kinsman Consulting team, where for many years he has continued to contribute to some of the valley’s most respected wines.

After countless seasons living the demanding yet rewarding rhythm of double harvests, bringing a Napa Valley wine into Kohue felt like a new horizon in our story.

Our Napa Valley Cabernet Sauvignon completes a circle between the renowned wine regions that have defined our vision, reflecting the essence of both our journey and the Spirit of Kohue Wines.

The Name
Kohue, pronounced Ko-[weh], is the name of a remarkable native wild plant in the indigenous language, a true protagonist of the flora, fauna, and culture of Mendoza. With its vibrant yellow flowers and captivating aroma, it adorns and perfumes the majestic landscape beneath the Andes.
Chosen and protected as the province’s official plant, it plays a vital role in sustaining our soils and is renowned for its various medicinal properties, discovered by our ancestors.
Just like the plant that inspires our name, Kohue Wines embodies a deep respect for nature, tradition, and sustainability—honoring these values every step of the way.
